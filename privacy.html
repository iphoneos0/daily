Privacy

Last updated November 26, 2026.

No accounts

You can play every game without signing up. We never ask for your name, email or any other personal details.

Scores on your device

Your best scores and the sound setting are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Visit statistics

We use Google Analytics to count visits, so we can see which games people enjoy. It records things like the pages you open, when a game starts and ends, your score, your device type and your approximate country. It does not record who you are. Advertising features and Google signals are switched off.

Visitors in the EU, the UK and Switzerland get no analytics cookies; Google estimates those visits without them. Elsewhere, Google Analytics may set a first-party cookie. You can block it with your browser settings or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Advertising

Some games show ads from Google AdSense: a short full-screen ad between rounds now and then, and optional videos you can choose to watch for an in-game reward. Ads never interrupt a round you are playing.

Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads in Google's Ads Settings, and learn more at aboutads.info. Visitors in the EU, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your device's own share sheet opens, or the text is copied to your clipboard. Nothing is posted anywhere unless you choose to.
